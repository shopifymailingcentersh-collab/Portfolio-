ABOUT PRIMAL LUX

Most freelancers learn tools.
I learned behavior.

Over the last 5 years, I've spent countless hours studying why customers buy, why stores fail silently, and why some brands feel premium before you even check the price.
Primal Lux was built from that understanding.

“Primal” represents instinct — the raw psychology behind attention, trust, emotion, urgency, and buying decisions.
“Lux” represents execution — clean design, premium presentation, smooth customer experience, and polished strategy.

Together, they create stores that don’t just look good. They convert.

What started as simple Shopify customization evolved into deep e-commerce problem solving: fixing conversion leaks, improving mobile experiences, reducing checkout friction, and helping brands stop losing revenue from invisible mistakes.
Today, every project is approached with one belief:
Small details create massive differences in revenue.
